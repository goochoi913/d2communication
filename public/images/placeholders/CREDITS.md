# Placeholder image credits

Temporary placeholder photography for the D2 Communication design demos. Every image will be
replaced with Jin Sam Choi's own stills before launch. All images come from Wikimedia Commons
under the licenses listed below.

**Changes made:** every image was cropped and resized from the original. Each one is provided
as a 1920×1080 `<slug>.webp` and an 800×450 `<slug>-sm.webp`, and some also have a 1080×1350
`<slug>-portrait.webp`. In `concert-stage-lights`, a small festival logo on the stage was
blurred. Images under CC BY-SA keep their original CC BY-SA license.

If an image appears publicly, show its credit line, for example in a credits page or a caption,
as `Author / Wikimedia Commons / License`. `manifest.json` has the same data in a
machine-readable form.
